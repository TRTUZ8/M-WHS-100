M-WHS: Myocardium Whole Heart Segmentation


Ground truth pixel-wise annotations were manually delineated on the CT slices by experienced medical image annotation experts following a standardized annotation protocol. The boundaries of the sinus of Valsalva, including the left coronary sinus, right coronary sinus, and noncoronary sinus , were manually delineated. All annotations were subsequently reviewed and validated by a perfusionist and a cardiologist, each with more than 20 years of clinical experience.

To ensure annotation consistency, a standardized annotation protocol was implemented. Delineation commenced at the basal ring and proceeded superiorly to the sinotubular junction, encompassing the sinus of Valsalva, including the left coronary, right coronary, and noncoronary sinuses, as completely as permitted by the available image data. Only the sinus region directly contiguous with the aortic wall was included in the annotations, whereas the ascending aorta beyond the sinotubular junction was excluded. The contours were traced along the luminal boundary defined by the aortic valve anatomy. Anatomically distinct boundaries were preserved between the left coronary, right coronary, and noncoronary sinuses to maintain accurate separation of the three sinus components. The ground truth annotations were encoded as voxel-wise segmentation masks, in which label 1 to the myocardium, label 2 to the aortic valve, and label 3 to the calcification.
